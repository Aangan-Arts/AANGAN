About AANGAN

Handmade Diwali décor and festive gifts

AANGAN makes handcrafted décor for Indian festivals and homes: diyas and diya holders, tealight holders, torans, Shubh Labh hangings, latkans and door hangings. Each piece is made by hand with pearls, mirror work, golden embellishments, stonework and embroidery.

Our pieces are made for Diwali, pooja rooms, festive tables, weddings and special occasions, and they make thoughtful festive gifts for family and friends. Want something made just for you? Request a custom creation and tell us what you have in mind.

Questions

How do I order from AANGAN?

Add pieces to your bag, or tap Buy now, and fill in your delivery details. WhatsApp opens with your order ready to send. We confirm availability, shipping and your total, then share a payment link.

When do I pay?

Only after we confirm your order on WhatsApp. Nothing is charged when you place an order on the website.

Is there a Diwali offer?

Yes. Every piece on offer is flat 20% off, and the offer price is shown on each one.

Are AANGAN pieces handmade?

Yes. Every piece is handcrafted, with details such as pearls, mirror work, golden embellishments, stonework and embroidery.

Do you deliver to my city?

We confirm delivery to your pincode, and the shipping cost, on WhatsApp before you pay.

Can I order a custom design?

Yes. Tap “Request a custom creation” at the bottom of the page and tell us what you have in mind, the occasion and your budget. WhatsApp opens with your request, and we reply with ideas and a price.

Are these good as Diwali gifts?

Yes. Diyas, tealight holders, torans and Shubh Labh hangings are traditional Diwali gifts, and you can add a gift note in the note field when you order.
