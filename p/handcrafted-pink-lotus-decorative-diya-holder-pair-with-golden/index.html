Care
  • Gently clean with a soft, dry cloth to remove dust.
  • Avoid washing or immersing the product in water to protect the decorative stones, mirror work, and golden finish.
  • Keep away from excessive moisture and humidity to maintain the product's appearance.
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight to preserve the vibrant pink color and decorative finish.
  • Do not use harsh chemicals, detergents, or abrasive cleaning materials.
  • Handle the lotus motifs and stone embellishments carefully to prevent damage or detachment.
  • Store in a cool, dry place inside a protective box when not in use.
  • If using with a diya, keep flames and heat away from decorative elements and any non-metal components. Never leave a burning diya unattended.
