Care
  • Gently remove dust using a soft, dry cloth or a soft-bristled brush.
  • Avoid water, excessive moisture, and harsh cleaning chemicals to protect the flowers and decorative finish.
  • Keep the artificial flowers and pearl embellishments away from direct heat and open flames.
  • If using tealight candles, ensure the candle flame does not come into direct contact with the holder to prevent damage.
  • Keep away from prolonged direct sunlight to preserve the vibrant colours and golden finish.
  • Handle the artificial flowers, pearl borders, and decorative elements carefully to prevent damage or detachment.
  • Store in a dry place in a protective box or fabric bag when not in use.
